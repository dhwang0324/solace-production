Our Story

We built Solace because great creative work shouldn't come with a $250k price tag.

Solace was founded in 2026 with a clear mission: give ambitious brands access to the same quality of creative direction, design, and production that large agencies charge a fortune for.

We work with startups, scale-ups, and established businesses across every industry. The common thread is ambition — clients who care deeply about how their brand shows up in the world.

Our process is transparent, our timelines are honest, and our craft is non-negotiable.

The Philosophy

Design without strategy is decoration. We start every engagement with questions, not Figma. Understanding your goals before we open a tool is what separates work that looks good from work that performs.

The Process

Discovery, direction, production, launch. Four clear phases with defined deliverables at every stage. You always know where your project stands — no black boxes, no surprises.

The Partnership

We measure success by your outcomes, not our output. That means staying involved after launch, tracking performance, and continuing to iterate as your brand grows.
